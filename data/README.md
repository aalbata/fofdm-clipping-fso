# Power records

`power_sun.csv` and `power_rain.csv` are derived from

> F. P. Guiomar, A. Lorences Riesgo, D. Ranzal, F. Rocco, A. Carena, and P. P. Monteiro,
> "Experimental characterization of optical received power in an outdoor free-space optics link,"
> Zenodo, 2020, doi: 10.5281/zenodo.3708496,

distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The variables `power` (received optical power in dBm)
and `samplingTime` (4 s) of the measurement files `2019-04-10_powerMeas_sun.mat` and
`2019-04-15_powerMeas_rain.mat` were written as text with the columns `time_s` (time since the first
sample) and `power_dbm` (power at the 0.01 dBm resolution of the measurement). The files remain
under the license of the dataset.
